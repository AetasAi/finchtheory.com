The Approach

The work of a commercial architect.

Most businesses would not build a new office without an architect, a blueprint and a plan. Yet many try to build growth without the same level of structure.

Book a Commercial Review See how it works →

The principle

I do not become your growth engine. I build yours.

Every engagement is designed to produce something your team can run, report on and improve independently. The goal is lasting capability, not ongoing dependency.

The role

Not a campaign. Not a hire. A commercial system.

Most businesses approach growth through campaigns or people. Both can work. Neither builds the infrastructure that makes growth repeatable regardless of who is in the room.

What businesses typically do

  • ✕Run campaigns — activity that stops when the budget does
  • ✕Hire BD or sales people — one person's capacity, no underlying system
  • ✕Rely on founders — growth dependent on one or two individuals
  • ✕Commission strategy documents — plans that describe the problem, not resolve it

What Finch Theory builds instead

  • ✓Commercial infrastructure that operates whether or not I am in the room
  • ✓A system the whole team can run, not a person doing the work
  • ✓Growth capability that survives changes in personnel
  • ✓A working Commercial Operating System™ — not a document

Finch Theory creates the commercial blueprint, then builds the infrastructure required to support sustainable growth.

The model

Fractional. Embedded. Finite.

I work alongside your existing team, not instead of them. The engagement has a defined shape and a clear end point. The goal is not ongoing dependency. It is lasting capability.

Alongside, not instead

I integrate with your current team and processes. I am not a replacement for your people or your marketing. I am the layer that gives your commercial activity structure, visibility and repeatability.

Building, not doing

The output is infrastructure your team can run, not a service they rely on me to deliver. Every piece of work is designed to transfer knowledge and capability, not to create dependency.

Defined and finite

Every engagement is scoped with a clear start, structured milestones and a defined end point. There is no open-ended retainer. The work finishes when the system is built and handed over to your team.

Left behind, not taken away

When the engagement ends, the system stays. Your data is in your CRM. Your processes are documented. Your team is trained. The Commercial Operating System™ belongs to your business.

The method

Three phases. One system.

Every engagement follows the same structure. The scope of each phase varies by business, but the sequence is consistent.

01

Identify

We map what already exists. Relationships, data, technology and process. We identify what is working, what is missing and where the highest commercial value sits. This is where the blueprint begins.

02

Build

We design and implement the Commercial Operating System™ across the five foundations. Practical, structured work with clear milestones and measurable outputs at each stage.

03

Activate

We hand over a working system and train your team to run it. The engagement closes with infrastructure that is adopted, understood and operating independently. Not a report. A capability.
